The Live Photo cleanup guide

How to convert Live Photos to still photos in bulk

Keep the still image and remove motion you no longer need. Live Photo Cleaner+ lets you review Live Photos individually or convert multiple selected photos with Bulk Mode.

Updated September 4, 2026 · For iPhone with iOS 17 or later

The essential detail: conversion creates a new still photo. The original Live Photo moves to Recently Deleted and continues to use storage until you permanently delete it or it expires.

Before you start

Choose photos whose motion and sound you no longer need. Keep irreplaceable Live Photos or back them up before permanently deleting their originals. Start with a small group so you can check the results.

The app includes 1 GB of free cleanup. Scanning and reviewing let you understand your library before deciding whether to purchase the optional lifetime unlock.

Scan, choose, and convert

  1. Open Live Photo Cleaner+ and allow photo access. The app can only review the library items you let it access. Limited access may hide photos you expected to see.
  2. Let the scan finish. Review the estimated recoverable storage. This is an estimate, not a guarantee of the change you will see in iPhone Storage.
  3. Choose Bulk Mode for multiple selections. Use the available filters and select the Live Photos you want to convert. For individual decisions, use Today’s Quick 10, a date, or an album in Swipe Review.
  4. Review the plan. Convert keeps a still replacement. Keep leaves the Live Photo as it is. Delete removes the photo rather than keeping a still replacement. Check the action selected for each photo.
  5. Confirm the cleanup. The app prepares the still replacements before moving converted originals to Recently Deleted. If an item cannot be converted, inspect the result and any reported error before trying again.

Finish reclaiming storage in Photos

Open Photos and check the new still images first. Then open Recently Deleted, unlock it if requested, and review the originals you intend to remove. Permanently delete only the items you are sure you no longer need; avoid clearing unrelated deleted photos by mistake.

Apple normally keeps deleted photos for 30 days. Once an original is permanently deleted, it cannot be recovered from Recently Deleted. You can instead wait for it to expire. The app cannot empty this album for you. Apple explains deletion and recovery.

What changes with iCloud Photos?

Some originals may need to download from iCloud before conversion can finish. Leave enough working space and a reliable connection for those downloads.

If iCloud Photos is enabled, changes to your library sync to other devices using the same account. Removing a Live original is a library change, not just a local cache cleanup. The still replacement can sync through Apple’s service too.

With Optimize iPhone Storage, the original video may not have been fully stored on your phone. That means the reduction in local device storage can differ from the app’s estimate. Allow Photos time to sync and update its storage calculation.

Photo quality and metadata

The app preserves the existing photo format where possible, including HEIF, and carries over supported information such as date, location, favorites, and editable album membership. A replacement is a new asset: some embedded metadata, editing history, or Live Photo effects may differ. Inspect photos with edits or special effects before removing their originals.

The still replacement does not contain the original Live Photo’s motion or sound. Recover the Live original from Recently Deleted if you change your mind while it remains available.

Want to stop taking new Live Photos?

Camera settings affect future photos. They do not convert the Live Photos already in your library. Read what turning off Live Photos does to storage, or visit the FAQ.
