Live Photos explained

Does turning off Live Photos save space?

Turning off Live while editing an existing photo is reversible. It does not perform the same cleanup as creating a still replacement and removing the original Live Photo.

Updated September 4, 2026

“Turn off Live Photos” can mean three different actions. The right one depends on whether you want to stop playback, change future photos, or reduce storage used by existing photos.

Three ways to change Live Photos
ActionWhat changesStorage effect
Turn Live off in PhotosThe existing photo stops playing as a Live Photo. You can turn Live back on.Do not rely on this to remove the stored motion.
Turn Live off in CameraNew photos are captured without Live motion while that setting is off.Can avoid the extra motion data in future photos; does not clean existing ones.
Convert to a still and remove the originalA still replacement stays in your library. The Live original is removed.Can reclaim the original’s extra storage after it leaves Recently Deleted. Actual savings vary.

Turning off playback is reversible

Apple lets you turn Live off or back on when editing a Live Photo. Because the motion can be restored, treat this as a playback/editing choice rather than a way to permanently remove the underlying video. Apple documents the toggle in Take and edit Live Photos.

Camera settings affect the next photo

Turn Live Photos off in Camera if you prefer to capture stills going forward. To preserve that choice, enable Live Photo under Settings → Camera → Preserve Settings, then turn Live off in Camera. Labels and placement can vary by iOS version. Existing Live Photos remain in your library.

To clean existing photos, keep a still replacement

Live Photo Cleaner+ scans your library, lets you choose which photos should keep their motion, and converts selected Live Photos into still replacements. You can review photos one by one or select multiple items in Bulk Mode.

Converted originals go to Recently Deleted. Check the replacements before removing those originals permanently, or wait for their recovery period to expire. Follow the full conversion guide.

How much space will I actually get back?

There is no fixed percentage that applies to every library. Savings depend on the original video, the replacement image, and whether the original was fully downloaded to the device. iCloud Photos with Optimize iPhone Storage can make local savings differ from the estimated amount.

For a useful check, record your device’s storage before cleanup, convert a small group, inspect the replacements, and finish the Recently Deleted step. Let Photos finish syncing before comparing the totals. Keep your own test results separate from a universal savings claim.

What if I change my mind?

As long as the Live original is still in Recently Deleted, you may be able to recover it in Photos. Recovery adds the original back and may leave both it and the still replacement in your library. After permanent deletion, the app cannot recreate the original motion. Read Apple’s recovery guidance.
