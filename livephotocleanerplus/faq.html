Live Photo Cleaner+ FAQ

Answers about conversion, storage, privacy, and the lifetime unlock.

What does Live Photo Cleaner+ do?

It finds Live Photos on your iPhone and lets you keep them, convert selected ones into still photos, or delete them. Conversion is designed to reduce storage used by motion you no longer need. It is not a general duplicate-photo detector.

Is it free?

The app is free to download and includes 1 GB of cleanup. You can buy a one-time lifetime unlock for unlimited cleaning. There is no subscription. The App Store and purchase screen show the current price for your country.

Can I convert Live Photos in bulk?

Yes. Use Bulk Mode to filter your Live Photos and select multiple items. Use Swipe Review when you prefer to decide one photo at a time, including Today’s Quick 10, dates, or albums.

Will I lose the still photo?

Convert prepares a still replacement before moving the original Live Photo to Recently Deleted. Delete is a different action: it does not keep a still replacement. Review the complete plan and inspect the converted photos before permanently removing originals.

When is storage actually freed?

Originals continue to use storage while they remain in Recently Deleted. Check the replacements, then permanently delete only the originals you intend to remove in Photos, or let them expire. Storage calculations may take time to update. The app cannot empty Recently Deleted.

Does it work with iCloud Photos?

Yes, but originals stored only in iCloud may need to download before conversion. Library changes sync through iCloud Photos to other devices using the same account. With Optimize iPhone Storage, local savings can differ from estimates because the original video may not have been fully downloaded.

Are my photos uploaded?

Live Photo Cleaner+ processes photos on your device and does not upload your library to a developer-operated server. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple’s own download and sync services still operate. The app does not require an account or use third-party tracking SDKs.

Are format and metadata preserved?

The app preserves format where possible, including HEIF, and supported information such as date, location, favorites, and editable album membership. A still replacement is a new asset. Some embedded metadata, editing history, or Live Photo effects may differ. Check important photos before removing originals.

Can I undo a conversion?

You can recover an original from Recently Deleted while Photos still offers recovery, normally for up to 30 days. This may leave both the original and the still replacement in your library. After permanent deletion, the app cannot restore the original motion or sound.

Which devices are supported?

Live Photo Cleaner+ is designed for iPhone and requires iOS 17.0 or later.

How do I restore my lifetime purchase?

Open Settings in the app and choose Restore Purchase while signed into the Apple account used for the purchase. Contact support if the unlock still does not appear.
