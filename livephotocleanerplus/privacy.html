Privacy policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Live Photo Cleaner+ requires no account and includes no ads, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. Photos and metadata are processed on your device, never uploaded by the app.

Photos and media

Photo access lets the app find Live Photos, estimate savings, show previews, and convert your selections using Apple’s Photos framework. Hidden photos are not scanned or cleaned.

The app sends no photos, videos, previews, albums, locations, dates, captions, or other photo metadata to the developer or a developer-operated server.

Local app data

Preferences and cleanup state—including appearance, free cleanup usage, review prompts, and purchase unlock state—may be stored on your device to operate app features. Delete the app to remove this local data.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase unlocks unlimited cleaning for life. Apple processes it through the App Store and StoreKit; the app uses Apple-provided purchase information to check your unlock. The developer never receives or stores payment card details.

Apple services

iCloud Photos and other Apple services handle storage, syncing, downloads, purchases, and account information under Apple’s privacy policies. Live Photo Cleaner+ does not operate these services.

Data sharing

The app does not sell data or share photos or personal data with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Policy changes

Updates to this policy will be reflected in the effective date above.
