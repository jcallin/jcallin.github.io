Live Photo Cleaner+ Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 23, 2026

Live Photo Cleaner+ is designed to help you review Live Photos and convert selected Live Photos into still images. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Summary

  • The app does not require an account.
  • The app does not include ads.
  • The app does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Your photos and photo metadata are processed on your device and are not uploaded by Live Photo Cleaner+ to a server.

Photos and Media

Live Photo Cleaner+ requests access to your photo library so it can find Live Photos, estimate potential storage savings, show previews, and convert the Live Photos you select. Photo processing happens locally on your device using Apple's Photos framework.

The app does not send your photos, videos, previews, albums, locations, dates, captions, or other photo metadata to the developer or to a developer-operated server. Hidden photos are not scanned or cleaned by the app.

Local App Data

Live Photo Cleaner+ may store app preferences and cleanup state on your device, such as appearance settings, free cleanup usage, review prompt state, and purchase unlock state. This data is used only to operate app features on your device.

You can delete local app data by deleting Live Photo Cleaner+ from your device.

In-App Purchases

Live Photo Cleaner+ offers a one-time in-app purchase for lifetime access to unlimited cleaning. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The app uses Apple-provided purchase information to determine whether unlimited cleaning is unlocked.

The developer does not receive or store your payment card details.

iCloud Photos and Apple Services

If you use iCloud Photos or other Apple services, Apple may handle photo storage, syncing, downloads, purchases, and account information according to Apple's own privacy policies. Live Photo Cleaner+ does not operate those services.

Data Sharing

Live Photo Cleaner+ does not sell your data and does not share your photos or personal data with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

Live Photo Cleaner+ is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. If changes are made, the effective date above will be updated.

Contact
