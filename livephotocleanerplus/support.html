Support

Live Photo Cleaner+ converts selected Live Photos to stills on your device to reclaim storage.

Missing photos

Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos and confirm the app has access to the photos you want to clean.

iCloud downloads

Some originals must download from iCloud before conversion. Allow enough working space and a reliable connection.

Recovering originals

Check Recently Deleted in Photos while recovery is still available. Permanently deleted originals cannot be restored.

Purchases

To restore your lifetime unlock after reinstalling, choose Restore Purchase in the app’s Settings using the Apple account that bought it.

Apple handles purchases, billing, and refunds. View purchase history or request help at reportaproblem.apple.com.
