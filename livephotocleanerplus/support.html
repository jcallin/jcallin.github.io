What Live Photo Cleaner+ Does

Live Photo Cleaner+ helps you review Live Photos and convert selected Live Photos into still images to reclaim storage. Photo processing happens on your device.

Common Help Topics

  • If the app cannot see expected photos, check iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos and confirm Live Photo Cleaner+ has the photo access you intended.
  • If iCloud Photos is enabled, some assets may need to download before they can be converted.
  • After conversion, original Live Photos may be recoverable for a limited time from Recently Deleted in the Photos app.
  • Use Restore Purchase in Settings if your lifetime unlock does not appear after reinstalling the app.
